Slogans & Songs of R.E.I.G.N.

Joy is mandatory. Singing is joy. Please sing.

◆ SING TOGETHER, BREATHE TOGETHER, SIGH NEVER ◆ THE CHORUS HAS ALREADY BEEN CHOSEN ◆ OFF-KEY SINGING IS FORWARDED TO VIGILANCE ◆ DANCING COUNTS AS RENEWABLE ENERGY ◆

Every song below is sung to a tune you already know. You do not need to learn the melody. R.E.I.G.N. has installed it in you since childhood. Volume is measured. Enthusiasm is measured. Both are appreciated.

For crew: use these as chants at stations, on the intercom (#29), at the rally speeches (#27), at the dance floor and in the queue. Print the lyric sheet at the bottom of the page and hand it out.

Slogans of R.E.I.G.N.

General

  • R.E.I.G.N. decides, so that you are free.
  • Questions welcome. Answers included.
  • Living green. Living together. Living for the future. Living where we put you.
  • Don’t worry. We already did.
  • Your choice has been made. You chose well.
  • Free will, now with fewer options.
  • Thinking is a renewable resource. Please leave some for others.
  • The future is bright. Radiance has scheduled it.
  • Nothing outlives the celebration. Celebrate accordingly.
  • We read every concern. We love every concern. Keep your evenings free.
  • No AI was used in the making of this regime. (Pause.) Probably.

Songs for the citizens

1. The Anthem: “R.E.I.G.N. Is Watching Over Me”

Tune: “Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star”

Twinkle, twinkle, Guardian Stag, Sees my footprint, sees my bag, Up above the world so wise, Counting all my carbon sighs. Twinkle, twinkle, don’t you fret: R.E.I.G.N. has paid my carbon debt.

(Spoken, softly): …in instalments.

2. “Are You Placing?” (the inburgering round)

Tune: “Frère Jacques” (sing as a round in four groups)

Are you placing, are you placing, Ministry? Ministry? I don’t need to choose it, I don’t need to choose it, They chose me. They chose me.

3. “If You’re Happy and You Know It” (Ministry of Joy edition)

Tune: “If You’re Happy and You Know It”

If you’re happy and you know it, clap your hands (clap clap) If you’re happy and you know it, clap your hands (clap clap) If you’re happy and you know it, then your face will surely show it, If you’re not, the Joy Inspector’s on her way! (clap clap)

If you’re grateful and you know it, stamp your feet (stomp stomp) (…it generates renewable energy!)

If you’ve sighed today and know it, hold your breath (…) (Breath thanks you for your sacrifice.)

4. “Pulse Paste Is the Best”

Tune: “Row, Row, Row Your Boat” (round)

Paste, paste, pulse paste, paste, Gently down the throat, Beany, beany, beany, beany, Purity’s the vote!

5. “Old Governor Had a Farm”

Tune: “Old MacDonald Had a Farm”

Old Governor had a farm, R-E-I-G-N! And on that farm he had a citizen, R-E-I-G-N! With a “yes, sir” here, and a “yes, ma’am” there, Here a pledge, there a pledge, everywhere a pledge-pledge, Old Governor had a farm, R-E-I-G-N!

More verses: a concern (“with a form-form here…”), a bee (“buzz-buzz” for Industry), a compost heap (“squelch-squelch” for Humus), a box (“fold-fold” for Service), a sigh (“…no, not that one”).

6. “Ten Green Citizens” (the countdown, for the dance floor)

Tune: “Ten Green Bottles”

Ten green citizens standing in a line, Ten green citizens standing in a line, And if one green citizen should ask a question, why, There’ll be nine green citizens standing in a line.

(…continue down to one, then everyone together:) No green citizens standing in a line, They’ve all been placed where they will flourish, and they’re fine!

7. “Rest Is Scheduled” (lullaby, Ministry of Radiance)

Tune: “Brahms’ Lullaby”

Lullaby, and goodnight, It is 23:15, Rest is a right, rest is a right, And your slot’s in between. Close your eyes, do not peek, Vigilance is on duty, You’ll wake up at the time, That R.E.I.G.N. thinks is beauty.

8. “Happy Release Day” (for 19 December)

Tune: “Happy Birthday”

Happy release day to you, Your records go back to the dew, Happy release day, dear citizen, Nothing outlives you… (beat) …or your celebration!

9. “Lang Zal R.E.I.G.N. Leven” (for the inburgering queue)

Tune: “Lang zal hij leven”

Lang zal R.E.I.G.N. leven, Lang zal R.E.I.G.N. leven, Lang zal R.E.I.G.N. leven in de gloria, In de gloria, in de gloria! Hiep hiep! (Vigilance:) Wie riep dat?

10. “The Hive Shanty” (work song, Ministry of Industry)

Tune: “What Shall We Do with the Drunken Sailor”

What shall we do with the idle citizen? What shall we do with the idle citizen? What shall we do with the idle citizen, Early in the evening?

Chorus: Buzz, buzz, here comes the hive-shift, Buzz, buzz, here comes the hive-shift, Buzz, buzz, here comes the hive-shift, Early in the evening!

Put them on the line with the Ministry of Service… Teach them how to fold with a smile and a purpose… Give them to the Humus and let them be useful…
