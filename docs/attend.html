Citizenship Intake & The Carbon Declaration

You were born owing the planet a little. Happily, R.E.I.G.N. accepts confessions.

◆ PLACES ARE GIVEN, NOT TAKEN ◆ ALL ORIGINS ARE WELCOME, EVENTUALLY ◆ EVERY ANSWER IS PAID FOR IN WATER ◆ R.E.I.G.N. HAS DONE THE CONVERSION ◆

How citizens are processed

  1. Apply online at join-ecotopia.org and sign in.
  2. Fill in the intake (below) and the carbon declaration.
  3. Receive a carbon debt number.
  4. Be assigned one ministry and one quest. You do not have to choose. That kindness has already been arranged.
  5. On the night, arrive 20:30 to 21:00 for your inburgering at the garage entrance, where you receive your passport.

Not applied? Processed at the door: a longer wait, and further obligations.
