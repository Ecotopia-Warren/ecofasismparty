Citizen passport: cut and fold

1. Cut (sheet flat, printed side up)

Lay a ruler from ● to ●: the two points in the middle where four pages meet. Cut only between them (under pages 4 and 3).

2. Fold in half lengthwise: top half down, BEHIND

Print stays on the outside. You now see a long strip: 6 · 7 · 8 · 1. The cut is in the top (folded) edge, in the middle.

3. Push both ends to the middle

Hold page 6 (left end) and page 1 (right end). Push them towards each other. The cut opens and pages 7 and 8 bulge out. Seen from above it becomes a diamond:

4. Keep pushing until it is a plus (+), then press flat

The ends meet in the middle. From above you see four arms. Fold the arms together into one stack.

5. Close it with page 1 on the front

Turn the stack so the cover (Republic of Ecotopia, Citizen Passport, page 1) is on the outside. Leaf through: 1, 2, 3 … 8. Page 8 (final balance) is the back. Press all folds hard.
