1. Objective

Select one of ten passport versions for a production run of 250 citizens (De Warren, 28 Nov 2026), printed on a single-sided laser printer and stamped with the small TPU stamp set (19.5 mm seals, 15 mm credits).

2. Hypotheses

  1. H1 A passport with 12 to 20 stamp boxes is enough for one night; more boxes add paper, not play.
  2. H2 Single-sided versions (01 to 05) cost less assembly time per passport than manual-flip versions (06 to 10).
  3. H3 Fold-up versions feel more like a real passport (immersion) than flat cards, at a cost in paper and time.
  4. H4 Every printed stamp box fits a 15 mm credit stamp without the impression touching the border.

6. Procedure

  1. Print one sheet of every version at 100%. S = single pass. D = manual flip: print side 1, put the stack back in the tray turned over, print side 2. Mark the top edge of the stack with a pencil first.
  2. Test 1 (pages 3 and 4): measure, check, and time each version with a stopwatch from blank sheet to finished passport.
  3. Test 2 (page 5): press every small-set stamp inside its outline.
  4. Abuse test: carry each passport in a pocket for an hour, stamp it 10 times, bend it, spill a drop of water on it.
  5. Fill in the summary below, accept or reject each hypothesis, and sign the verdict on page 6.
