CREW ONLY · STATION #20

SHOOTING RANGE
OPERATOR BRIEF

The Gallery of Gentle Correction · location: TBD · operator: Chris

1 · SET UP (before 20:30)

  • Glass out of every frame. Box the glass and put it away, far from the range. No glass in the room.
  • Frames fixed to the wall (screws, hooks or strong tape), at eye height. A frame must not fall when hit. Targets: 8 enemies, the Guardian and a Governor in between them.
  • Tape the firing line at 3 m from the wall. Nobody walks between the line and the wall: put the queue behind the line, the passage elsewhere.
  • Kit: foam darts only (count them, 5 per citizen), credit stamp + ink, pen, the station sign at the line.

2 · ONE ROUND

  1. One citizen at the line. Check the line is clear. Hand over 5 darts.
  2. Say “Line is yours.” They shoot all 5. Everyone else waits behind the line.
  3. Last dart gone: call “HOLD”. Nobody shoots. You alone fetch the darts and count the hits as you pull them.
  4. Back behind the line, all darts in hand: call “CLEAR”. Only now may the next citizen step up.
  5. Stamp the passport (p. 6–7 credit boxes). One round, then they rejoin the queue.

3 · SCORING (same as the sign)

100bull
50ring
25portrait
−100Governor
−200Guardian

Frame or wall: 0. A dart counts where it hangs or first hit; your call is final. Round total below 0 counts as 0: a bad round never takes credits away. Stamps = round total ÷ 100, rounded down, max 3.

100 + 50 + 25 + 25 + miss= 2002 stamps
100 + 100 + 50 − 100 (Governor) + miss= 1501 stamp
25 − 200 (Guardian) + 3 misses= -175 → 00 stamps

4 · SAFETY STOPS: call “HOLD” at once if

anyone steps past the line or between line and wall · a dart is aimed at a person, face or animal · a frame comes loose or glass shows up · a dart is hard, bent or modified (take it out) · a citizen is very drunk, aggressive or upset (go out of character, call Safety). OZONE from anyone: all play around that person stops, drop character: “Are you OK?” Taking part is voluntary. Range closes for the Grand Assembly (TBD: 23:15 or 23:30).
