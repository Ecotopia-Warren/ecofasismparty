GENTLE CORRECTION

Every dart is a small act of care. R.E.I.G.N. does the aiming, so that you need not.
  1. Stand behind the line: 3 metres. Foam darts only, from the range official.
  2. 5 darts per citizen per round. One round, then rejoin the queue.
  3. Hit an enemy of the green state: bull, ring, or anywhere on the portrait. Frame or wall: nothing.
  4. DO NOT SHOOT the official portraits. A Governor costs 100 CC, the Guardian 200 CC, off this round.

SAFETY & CONSENT

Aim only at the frames. Never at faces, people or animals.
Wait behind the line until the official calls CLEAR. Only the official fetches darts.
The official's call is final. Taking part is voluntary: you may step out at any time.
A bad round never costs you credits you already have. R.E.I.G.N. only adjusts the round.
