# Ecotopia — Canon Sheet (baseline: join-ecotopia.org)

Source of truth: https://join-ecotopia.org (pulled 2026-11-25). Anything in this repo that contradicts this sheet gets purged.
Items marked **[SITE]** are verbatim/near-verbatim from the website. Items marked **[DRAFT]** are invented to fill gaps and need sign-off.

## The world [SITE]

- **Ecotopia**: "One night in the last sustainable utopia." Tagline: *Living green, living together, living for the future.*
- **R.E.I.G.N.**: *Regime for Ecological Integrity and Global Nurturing*. "R.E.I.G.N. decides, so that citizens are free." The guiding hand, "so that no citizen ever has to wonder what comes next."
- **Voice**: soft, maternal, cozy-coercive. Never threatens; always has already decided, for your own good. ("Questions are welcome. They have already been answered.")
- **The Guardian**: a great stag watches over Ecotopia. "It sees everything, and it forgives most of it."
- **AI is banned** by decree of R.E.I.G.N.; the site "was made entirely by devoted human hands."
- **Governors** read every concern. Concerns are "forwarded to the Ministry of Vigilance. Please keep your evenings free."
- **Records**: every citizen record "is released back to nature" on 19 December 2026. "Nothing outlives the celebration."

## The Articles of Nurturing [SITE]

"Adopted unanimously. Every vote was counted, including yours."

1. Every citizen is placed where they will flourish. R.E.I.G.N. does the placing, so that you need not.
2. Questions are welcome. They have already been answered.
3. Rest is a right of every citizen. It is scheduled.
4. R.E.I.G.N. hears every concern. Every single one.

## The night [SITE]

- Saturday 28 November, 21:00 to 04:00. Arrive 20:30 to 21:00 for your **inburgering**.
- Location secret, disclosed to processed citizens. Not applied: "processed at the door: a longer wait, and further obligations."
- One donation covers everything inside.
- "Places are given, not taken. Dancing counts as renewable energy."
- "Until around midnight it is a game and a party at once: ministries, missions and a little intrigue. After that, it is just a party."
- Guests can step out of the game at any time (terms, placeholder).

## Citizenship intake [SITE]

Apply online → sign in → **carbon declaration** → a **carbon debt** number ("You were born owing the planet a little. Happily, R.E.I.G.N. accepts confessions.").
Each citizen is then assigned **one ministry and one quest** ("You do not have to choose. That kindness has already been arranged.").

Intake fields: full name, phone, country of origin ("All origins are welcome, eventually"), occupation in the old world ("R.E.I.G.N. will find a better use for it"), *what you would surrender first, for the good of all*, a one-sentence pledge, companions you'd like to serve alongside (names only; R.E.I.G.N. "hears every request, and decides"), how you were recruited / who recruited you.

Carbon declaration questions: meat on your plate (*"The Ministry of Purity recommends pulse paste"*), flights last year, relationship with the combustion engine ("I am, spiritually, a car"), screens and gadgets ("It is listening now"), lithium you keep close, houseplants you let die, questions asked to AI per day, times you said you are "basically vegan". "Every answer is paid for in water. R.E.I.G.N. has done the conversion."

## Teams [DESIGN DOC 2026-09-30 + SITE; DRAFT where marked]

Two kinds of team. Do not mix them.

### Guest teams: the ministries
- **Placement** [SITE]: every citizen is assigned **one ministry and one quest** at intake. "You do not have to choose. That kindness has already been arranged." Guests may name companions; "R.E.I.G.N. hears every request, and decides."
- **Size** [DRAFT]: ten ministries, ~20 citizens each at ~200 guests.
- **Entry** [DESIGN]: a citizen first works off their starting carbon debt; once debt-free they start contributing to their ministry.
- **Self-organising** [DESIGN]: each ministry has to work out who is in it, what it is about and what it is trying to achieve. Nobody hands them a leader or a plan.
- **Competition** [DESIGN]: ministries compete to generate the most power as efficiently as possible while staying sustainable. One currency (carbon credits) moves the same number for individuals and ministries.
- **The dilemma engine** [DESIGN]: winning pushes ministries into overusing resources or making inhumane calls, always framed positively. That tension is the point of ministry play.
- **The gathering** [DESIGN]: ministries are told all night to be "as ready as possible". At the climax R.E.I.G.N.'s leader arrives and every ministry must show what it built (a song, a chant, a formation) as if for judgment. This crowd is what the resistance raids.
- **Cross-cutting layer** [DESIGN]: ~10 personal quests seed the resistance. Rebels stay in their ministry; nobody can tell whose quest came from which pool.
- **Ministries and stations**: crew runs the stations; ministries govern themselves. Ministries **visit** stations and can choose to **support** them (send citizens to help, take on a station's tasks as ministry work, earn credits for it). A station is **not** the head of any ministry: station crew has no authority over a ministry or its citizens, and a ministry decides for itself whether and how it helps.

- Regime actors (police, interrogation, intercom voice, the leader's arrival) are crew playing R.E.I.G.N., not guests.
