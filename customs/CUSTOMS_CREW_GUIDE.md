## 2. Flow per guest (target 60–90 s)

1. **Greeter (≈10 s):** *Applied?* Yes → name → index → **"Desk 3."** No → **Erik**.
2. **Greet + identity (15 s):** name → bundle → check the name on page 2 → **tick** the ministry list.
3. **Declaration (10 s):** the customs line. Every answer is "noted by Light". Nothing is taken.
4. **Stamps (15 s):** p. 2 **ENTRY** box = ministry seal (DEFAULT) · *Processed at* = the time · *Officer* = your initials · p. 3 **VERDICT = APPROVED** if your desk has it (DEFAULT). Card not sealed yet? Seal it.
5. **Hand over (10 s):** passport + quest card. *"It scores for the ministry on the seal."*
6. **The 3 things (10 s):** **debt on p. 2 · 1 stamp = 100 CC · top 3 ministries compete at 23:30** · safe word **OZONE**.
7. **Point (5 s):** **Ministry goal setting, beside the bar** (DEFAULT). **"Next!"**

**Secret roles:** pitch to a guest who seems up for it; on a yes the Float hands **H0 Hacker** / **S0 Smuggler** face down, away from the queue. A role out means its **H1 / S1** card must be in circulation.
**Walk-ins:** Erik asks the declaration questions, sets the debt (DEFAULT 200–300 CC; applied guests mostly have 100–200, max 500), stamps **INTERROGATION · ANSWERED** + **VERDICT · FURTHER OBLIGATIONS** (DEFAULT) → Desk 6 fills in a blank, logs reg. no. / ministry / debt, adds the name to that ministry's list → that ministry's desk for the seal.

## 3. What to say

*R.E.I.G.N. voice: soft, motherly, already decided. Never threaten, never get angry; at most a little disappointed.*

<!-- card:start -->
**Greeter:** "Good evening, citizen. Did you apply?" *(Aangemeld?)* → "Your name? … Desk 3. R.E.I.G.N. was expecting you."

**Queue briefing, every ~3 min:** "Welcome to Ecotopia. Three things, so you never have to wonder. **One:** your passport shows your carbon debt. You were born owing the planet a little. **Two:** every good deed earns a stamp; one stamp is **100 credits**. Debt-free? Then your stamps serve your ministry. **Three:** at **23:30** only the **top 3 ministries** may compete for Ascension. And one word stops all play: **OZONE.**"

**Desk:**

- "Welcome, citizen. Your name, please." *(Welkom, burger. Uw naam, alstublieft.)* … "Ah, there you are."
- "Anything to declare for the Ministry of Light? Plastic? Lithium? A combustion engine? More than you can carry lightly?" *(Iets aan te geven?)*
- Any answer: "Thank you. Light has weighed it. You may keep it, for now." Phone: "It is listening now."
- "Your passport. Keep it visible. And your quest: you do not have to choose. That kindness has already been arranged."
- "Debt on page 2. One stamp, a hundred credits. Top three ministries, half past eleven." *(Schuld op pagina 2. Eén stempel is 100 credits. Top 3 om half twaalf.)*
- "Your ministry gathers beside the bar. Find them. They don't know you yet. Next!" *(Volgende!)*

**Edge cases:**

- **Not on the list:** "All origins are welcome, eventually. A few questions first, this way please." → Erik.
- **Any question:** "Questions are welcome. They have already been answered."
- **Late (after 21:00):** "The Guardian noticed, and forgives most of it. A longer wait, and further obligations."
- **Secret role:** "A special placement: a secret role, all night. You may be hunted. Interested?" Yes: "Hide it, tell no one. Caught means playing along, never dragged. You can always say no."

**Out of character** (hand flat on head):

- **Wants out:** "Out of game: no problem. Bar, toilets, dance floor are yours. Want back in? Come to the gate." *(Prima, je stapt uit het spel. Kom gerust terug.)*
- **Drunk / aggressive / upset:** "Out of game for a sec. Let's get you some water." Call Safety.
- **OZONE:** "Out of game. Are you OK? What do you need?"
